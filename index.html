Frequently Asked Questions

How is my GPA calculated?
SLIIT uses a 4.0 grading scale. Your GPA is calculated by multiplying the Grade Point Value (GPV) of each module by its credit value, summing these products, and then dividing by the total number of credits attempted.

GPA = Σ (Grade Point × Credit Value) / Σ Total Credits
Does this calculator support all SLIIT faculties?
Yes, this tool supports grading schemes for Computing, Engineering, Business, Architecture, and Humanities & Sciences faculties. It is updated according to the 2025 Student Handbook.
What are the passing grades at SLIIT?
Grade C and above are considered passing grades. Grades C- and below are considered failing grades.
What is the difference between Semester GPA and Weighted GPA?
Semester GPA (SGPA) reflects your performance in a single semester. Weighted GPA (WGPA or CGPA) is the cumulative average of all your semesters. Note that Computing specializations CS and CSE follow a weighted GPA system from 1st year onwards, whereas other Computing programs do not apply it on first year.
